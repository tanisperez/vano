Vano design system
Postino's tokens, metrics and components, extended for a disk analyzer.
Surfaces, text, states, radii and type come straight from theme/palette.rs and theme/metrics.rs. New here: three accent palettes, six category colors for the treemap, two cleanup treatments, and the motion rules for the scan. Light or dark follows the system through Theme::sync_system_appearance, the same call Postino uses.
