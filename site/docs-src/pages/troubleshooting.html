Troubleshooting

Part of the disk shows as Not scanned

Vano counts only what your user can read. System folders and other users' files are left out. On macOS, system folders also need Full Disk Access: open System Settings, Privacy and Security, Full Disk Access, and turn on Vano.

The scan of a WSL distribution fails

  • Check that the distribution starts: run wsl -d <name> in a terminal.
  • Its drives must be mounted under /mnt, the default: Vano runs its program from there.
  • Starting a stopped distribution can take a while on a slow or busy machine. Scan it again once it runs.

Moving to the Trash is slow

The Trash moves whole items, and on some systems copies them. For many thousands of files, delete them permanently instead: it is faster and frees the space at once.

The disk is not freer after cleaning up

Items in the Trash still take their space until you empty it. Inside a WSL distribution, the virtual disk does not shrink by itself (see WSL 2 distributions).

Reporting a bug

Set the log level to Debug in Settings, Advanced, or start Vano with VANO_LOG=debug, reproduce the problem, and attach the log (see Files and folders) to an issue on GitHub.
