Quick start

Scan your home folder, look at what fills it and move the safe recommendations to the Trash.

  1. Start Vano. The home screen lists your disks and folders. Home folder is selected: click Scan Home folder at the bottom right, or press Enter.
  2. While it scans, the treemap grows live and the panel on the right lists recommendations as their folders finish. A home folder usually takes a second or two.
  3. When it ends, the results open. Click a tile to open that folder, and right click it to see its details. Backspace goes back up.
  4. In Recommendations, the Safe ones are already selected. Read what each one is, untick what you want to keep, and click Delete.
  5. The confirmation lists what goes and offers two ways: Move to Trash, which you can undo, or Delete permanently, which frees the space at once. Confirm.

The scan is saved. Next time, Open results in the home screen's Recent list shows it again without scanning.
