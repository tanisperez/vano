Files and folders

Vano writes only these files. Deleting any of them is harmless: it starts over with defaults, or scans again.

WhatLinuxmacOSWindows
Settings, settings.toml~/.config/vano/~/Library/Application Support/vano/%APPDATA%\vano\
Recent scans, recents.json~/.config/vano/~/Library/Application Support/vano/%APPDATA%\vano\
Saved scans~/.cache/vano/scans/~/Library/Caches/vano/scans/%LOCALAPPDATA%\vano\scans\
Logs, vano.log~/.local/state/vano/~/Library/Logs/Vano/%LOCALAPPDATA%\Vano\logs\
Downloaded updates~/.cache/vano/updates/~/Library/Caches/vano/updates/%LOCALAPPDATA%\vano\updates\
The WSL program%LOCALAPPDATA%\Vano\wsl\

The log is rotated at 5 MB, keeping the last 10 files. The Linux paths follow XDG_CONFIG_HOME, XDG_CACHE_HOME and XDG_STATE_HOME when they are set.
