title: Settings description: Every setting of Vano: language, theme, accent, fonts, the live view, logging and updates. ---

Settings

Open them with the gear of the title bar, or Ctrl ,. Changes apply at once and are saved to settings.toml (see Files and folders).

Appearance

SettingWhat it does
LanguageEnglish, Spanish, Galician or Italian. Automatic follows your system.
ThemeLight, dark, or System, which follows your system and switches live.
AccentIndigo, Graphite or Electric.
Font and font sizeThe interface font. Geist is bundled, so every system looks the same.

Scanning

The live view a scan starts with: treemap, rings or ranking. The results open in the view the scan was showing.

Advanced

The log level: Off, Error, Warn, Info, Debug or Trace. Debug and Trace add detail to diagnose problems. Open folder shows the log file. The VANO_LOG environment variable overrides the level for one run.

About

The version, the system and where Vano is installed. When the build has an updater, the automatic update check, Check now, and the update when one is ready. See Updates.
